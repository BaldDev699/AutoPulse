About Us

H & L Store was founded with a simple goal: to make high-quality automobile spare parts easily accessible to vehicle owners, mechanics, and businesses across Kenya. Over the years, we have built a strong reputation based on trust, reliability, and excellent customer service.

We understand how important your vehicle is to your daily life and business operations. That’s why we carefully source our products from trusted manufacturers and suppliers to ensure durability, safety, and performance.

  • ✔ Brake systems and suspension parts
  • ✔ Electrical components and batteries
  • ✔ Tires, wheels, and exterior accessories
